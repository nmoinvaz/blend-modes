# Blend Modes: Symmetry, Generating Families, and an Atlas

Separable image **blend modes** — the per-channel `f(A, B)` behind Multiply, Screen, Difference, and
dozens more. Two ideas:

1. They come in **symmetry pairs** under the De Morgan dual `D·f = 255 − f(255−A, 255−B)`.
2. They're generated by a **few parametric families** (power means, Frank t-norms, dodge/burn powers),
   not dozens of unrelated formulas.

The atlas renders ~70 modes — FFmpeg's set, Krita's, and dual/predicted completions — grouped by family,
each beside its symmetric partner. Main image: landscape (A) over dog (B); the caption swatch is the same
mode on a hue-gradient × diagonal-bands reference. From [`scripts/blend_all.py`](scripts/blend_all.py).

## Conventions

`A` = top, `B` = bottom, 8-bit (`MAX = 255`); normalized forms use `a, b ∈ [0,1]`. Every symmetry claim
is checked **exhaustively** over all 256×256 inputs.

## Two symmetries

- **DUAL** `D·f = 255 − f(255−A, 255−B)` — complement dual; maps a darkening mode to its lightening mirror.
- **SWAP** `S·f = f(B,A)` — exchange layers.

`S` and `D` commute and self-invert → `{I, S, D, SD}` is a Klein four-group. Adobe's *PDF Blend Modes
Addendum* states two: `Screen = D(Multiply)`, `Overlay = HardLight(B,A)`.

| dark end | light end (= DUAL) | family |
|---|---|---|
| multiply | screen | multiplicative |
| darken | lighten | power-mean |
| burn | dodge | dodge/burn |
| difference | **phoenix** (= Krita *Equivalence*) | difference |
| negation | extremity | difference |
| exclusion | **inclusion** | difference |
| reflect | freeze | quadratic |
| glow | heat | quadratic |
| xor | xnor | bitwise |

`phoenix = 255 − |A−B| = D(difference)`. Mid-gray-centered modes (normal, average, overlay, the contrast
modes) are **self-dual**.

## Generating families

Most modes are samples from a few one-parameter formulas.

**Power means** (averaging axis): `M_p(a,b) = ((aᵖ + bᵖ)/2)^(1/p)`

| p | −∞ | −1 | →0 | 1 | 2 | +∞ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | darken | harmonic | geometric | average | rms | lighten |

**Frank t-norm** (one knob `s`): `T_s = log_s(1 + (sᵃ−1)(sᵇ−1)/(s−1))`, dual conorm `S_s = 1 − T_s(1−a,1−b)`.

| construction | s→0 | s=1 | s→∞ |
|---|---|---|---|
| t-norm `T_s` | darken | multiply | linearburn |
| t-conorm `S_s` | lighten | screen | addition |
| midpoint fuse | pinlight | hardlight | linearlight |
| symmetric difference | — | exclusion | difference |

**Dodge/burn** (one knob `k`): `Dodge_k = min(1, bᵏ/(1−a))`, `Burn_k = max(0, 1−(1−b)ᵏ/a)` → colordodge/burn
(k=1), glow/heat (k=2), cubic (k=3).

No single formula covers everything (modes differ in monotonicity, identity element, continuity), but these
three regenerate ~25 modes plus every blend between. See
[`verify_unify.py`](scripts/verify_unify.py), [`verify_families.py`](scripts/verify_families.py).

## Bitwise = the same logic, different algebra

`A + B − 2·∧(A,B)` is the whole difference family, varying the conjunction `∧`: `min → difference`,
`product → exclusion`, **bitwise AND → xor** (carry identity `A+B = (A⊕B) + 2(A∧B)`).

## Prior art

Most modes already exist; **Krita's set is the most exhaustive** (~70) and fuzzy-logic covers the t-norms.
Each tile shows our name, the **Krita** name (`K:`), and the **math** name (`M:`).

- Krita: `nand/nor/xnor`, geometric/harmonic (*Parallel*), p-norm, hard overlay, interpolation, quadratic
  fuses, gamma/penumbra/easy/soft-light.
- Fuzzy-logic research: Einstein/Hamacher/Yager t-norm blending.
- `phoenix` = Krita *Equivalence*; `reflect/glow/freeze/heat` from
  [Pegtop / Jens Gruschel](https://www.pegtop.net/delphi/articles/blendmodes/).

Not found anywhere: `inclusion` (dual of exclusion) and the exotic means (contraharmonic, logarithmic,
Heronian, identric, centroidal). The contribution is the **systematic symmetry + family treatment**, not
the individual modes.

## Benchmarks (SIMD)

SIMD cost tracks the taxonomy: linear/min-max/bitwise are ~free (1 instruction), multiplicative needs
multiply + `÷255`, dodge-burn/quadratic need a reciprocal, gamma/trig/means are transcendental.
[`bench/`](bench/) measures it with **Google Benchmark + CMake** (Apple Silicon NEON; median of 5,
L1-resident, scalar baseline with auto-vectorization off; every SIMD path bit-exact, polynomial ±1).

```sh
cmake -S bench -B bench/build && cmake --build bench/build && ./bench/build/blend_bench
```

**Tier 1/2 — scalar vs hand-NEON** (16-wide ~7 Gi/s; but see *Optimizations* — that's not the real ceiling):

| mode | scalar | NEON | speedup |
|---|---|---|---|
| darken / lighten / subtract / average / xor / diff | ~0.85 Gi/s | ~6.8 Gi/s | **~8×** |
| add (saturating) | 1.43 Gi/s | 6.75 Gi/s | 4.7× |
| multiply, screen | ~0.9–1.1 Gi/s | ~6.7 Gi/s | 6–8× |

(With auto-vectorization *on*, the compiler already NEONs the trivial ops; hand-intrinsics matter most for
saturating-add, the `÷255` modes, and Tier 3/4.)

**Tier 3 — float-reciprocal, hand-vectorized** (least prior art): `reflect/glow/heat/freeze` and the means
`geometric/harmonic/rms/contraharmonic` as 4-wide NEON float — **~2.7–3.1×** (~0.5–0.7 → ~1.5 Gi/s),
bit-exact, and HDR-capable (no LUT).

**Tier 4 — Frank t-norm, three ways** (one formula covers multiply/screen/min/max/linearburn/addition +
contrast fuses + difference/exclusion):

| Frank `s=10` | throughput | needs |
|---|---|---|
| scalar (`pow,pow,log,log`) | 104 Mi/s | libm |
| **NEON polynomial (gather-free)** | **700 Mi/s** (~6.7×) | FMAs only; float/HDR-capable |
| 256×256 LUT (indexed loads) | 1.4 Gi/s (~13×) | 64 KB table; 8-bit only |

`s` is constant per image, so one LUT (or one polynomial kernel) covers the whole Frank family and the trig
modes — swap the table, same kernel.

**Trig vectorizes too** — the best transcendental case: a degree-7 `atan` polynomial + `atan2` range
reduction (`min/max` ratio into `[0,1]`, then a branchless `vbslq` select for the `b>a` half-plane) gives
**`arctan` at 1.1 Gi/s, ~17× over scalar `atanf`** — bit-exact and gather-free.

### Optimizations tried

- **Unroll the cheap ops 4× → ~4× across all of Tier-1** (`add`/`darken`/`xor`/`diff`/`average` all
  ~7 → ~30 Gi/s, bit-exact). The 16-wide loop is memory-level-parallelism limited, not L1-bandwidth limited;
  four independent load/store streams expose the real ceiling.
- **Unrolling the Tier-3 float modes barely helps** (reflect/harmonic ~1.1×, geometric flat) — the
  divide/sqrt *unit* is throughput-bound, so ~1.5–1.9 Gi/s is near its ceiling.
- **Reciprocal / rsqrt estimate + Newton *backfires* on Apple Silicon** — native `vdivq`/`vsqrtq` win
  (reflect 1.43 → 1.42, geometric 1.90 → 1.32 Gi/s). The classic embedded-ARM trick is a pessimization here.
- **Unrolling the Frank polynomial is flat** (~0.76 → 0.73 Gi/s) — already enough ILP, compute-bound.
